Good to know

Is this a legal document?

No. This is a sample template to get you started. The terms are examples — review them (or have a lawyer look at them) before using them with a real client.

How is the price calculated?

The total is your price minus any discount. The payment schedule then splits it by your chosen terms — 50/50, 100% upfront, 30/40/30 milestones, or Net 14 — so the deposit is always right.

What if I leave the scope empty?

The service type you picked supplies a sensible default scope. Add, remove or rewrite lines — one deliverable per line, up to 12.

Is my client data stored anywhere?

No. Everything in this demo runs locally in your browser. Close the tab and it is gone.
